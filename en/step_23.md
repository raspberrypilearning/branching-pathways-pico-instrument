## 2D - Wrap stitch and stuff

Try fabric, felt or foam around your playing movement.

## Step 1

> [!TASK]
>
> Fold or wrap your material. Try squeezing, bending or wearing it before fixing anything in place.

## Step 2

> [!TASK]
>
> Make a firm, removable card pocket for the Pico. Keep USB accessible and leave slack for moving wires.

## Step 3

> [!TASK]
>
> Try a control through the covering. Foil contacts meet only when pressed; a light sensor needs an opening. Ordinary thread is not a signal wire. Keep conductive threads apart.

## Test

> [!TASK]
>
> Can you still open the body to fix a wire? Does the control work through the covering?

Use the wiring and code from your chosen control page in group 5.
