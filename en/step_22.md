## 2C - Adapt something you have

Turn a clean, dry object into part of your instrument.

## Step 1

> [!TASK]
>
> Choose an object you may change. Ask before using someone else's. Try how it sits, opens or moves.

## Step 2

> [!TASK]
>
> Use paper shapes to plan where controls could go. Can you reach them without pulling wires?

## Step 3

> [!TASK]
>
> Support the Pico on removable card. Keep USB accessible and cover metal that could touch the circuit.

## Test

> [!TASK]
>
> Play the movement. Move a control if your fingers cannot reach it easily.

For the circuit, choose any control page in group 5 and follow its wiring diagram and code.
