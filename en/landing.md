## What you will make

Make an instrument with a Raspberry Pi Pico. Its funny noises are yours to choose: beeps, notes, slides or rhythms. You also choose how to play it and what it looks like.

First, open the [starter project](resources/instrument.py) and complete [1A - Connect and test your Pico](1A%20-%20Connect%20and%20test%20your%20Pico.md). Once its LED and ready message work on your real Pico, choose any part:

- **Make your controls:** press, touch, turn, tip, move in the air and more. Pick one or several.
- **Choose a sound maker:** use an active buzzer for beeps or a passive buzzer for changing pitches.
- **Decide with code:** join your control and sound test scripts. Try notes, rhythms or more controls when you want.
- **Make the body:** start with a shape, material or playing movement.

You can craft, wire or code first after the Pico check. The page numbers group ideas; they are **not** a list to complete in order. If a page needs a part you have not tested yet, follow its link back and return when you are ready. The four Pico instrument ideas are examples to borrow from, not prescribed builds.

When it makes a sound you can control, play it, change one thing, and save your version. Notes about your work are optional.
