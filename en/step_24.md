## 7A - Play and change it

Your first playable version can be simple: one action makes a noise you chose.

> [!TIP]
>
If the sound does not follow your control yet, go back to [6A - Try one control](6A%20-%20Try%20one%20control.md), [6B - Give two controls a job](6B%20-%20Give%20two%20controls%20a%20job.md), or [6C - Put contacts wherever you want](6C%20-%20Put%20contacts%20wherever%20you%20want.md). Their wiring diagrams and code can help you find what is missing.

## Step 1

> [!TASK]
>
Run your own saved `instrument.py` in Thonny. Try every action. Stop the program before moving a wire.

## Step 2

> [!TASK]
>
Play a rhythm, strange noise or tiny tune. Change **one** thing: a note, timing, control position or part of the body. Play again. Keep the change if you like it.

## Test

> [!TASK]
>
Can someone else make a sound with your instrument? Save this version of your code. You can keep making another version whenever you want.

You can write a note, tell someone what changed, show a performance, or just keep building. None of those is a test you have to pass.
